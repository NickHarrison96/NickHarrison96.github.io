Right to Repair · Open Source

You Own It. You Should Understand It.

Computers are meant to be tools of empowerment, creative expression, and personal agency. Here's what we stand for.

  • You own it, so you deserve to understand it.Owning a device should mean you're allowed to look inside its software, learn how it boots, and get your own data back when something breaks.
  • Knowledge should be free.We publish what we find, free and open, so nobody has to learn it alone. No paywall, no account.
  • We show our work.Every command is the one we ran, and the code is on GitHub. If it helped us, it should be able to help you.
