02 / Before you contact us

A few details beat a vague diagnosis.

  1. Device name and exact model, if you know it.
  2. What happens, and what you expected instead.
  3. What changed before it stopped working.
  4. Repairs or recovery attempts already made.
  5. Whether the data matters, and if you have a backup.

Keep private things private.

Do not email passwords, unlock codes or private files. If data is at risk, stop experimenting before making more changes.

Local drop-off and remote/software help are available. Get in touch before bringing a device.
