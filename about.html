About

Who We Are.

We're your local hub for truth and support with all things tech: your open-source buddy. Harrison Technology Solutions is built on one stubborn idea: if you paid for it, you should be able to understand it, own it and keep it. We research how devices really work and share all of it, free and open.

I'm Nick, a security researcher and tech enthusiast. I explore how devices work behind the scenes and how they protect your personal data every day. Through that research, I help companies and their teams make the devices you rely on safer and more secure.

Where This Started

It started with stubbornness and a belief that the knowledge locked inside every device belongs to the person holding it, not the company that sold it. Self-taught, with more determination than money, Nick studied his way into how devices boot, lock and fail. We've been handing that knowledge back ever since, one write-up at a time.

What We Do

We do two things, and they're really the same thing: we research how devices work, and we publish everything we learn.

  • Bootloader & Data Recovery ResearchLow-level bootloader work and non-destructive recovery across iOS, Android, Windows and macOS. Case studies →
  • Open-Source ToolsRootForgeKit and Pymobile3-GUI. Read the code, run it, make it yours. Tools →
  • Write-ups & TutorialsStep-by-step guides so the next person doesn't have to learn it alone. Write-ups →

Why We Share Everything

Knowledge behind a paywall is just another locked door. So the tools are open source and the write-ups are free. If something we share helps you understand your own device, that's the whole point. If it doesn't make sense, ask us. We've got your back.

Working with us. Questions, research collaborations and press all start with an email. The fastest way to reach us is harrisontechdev@gmail.com.
