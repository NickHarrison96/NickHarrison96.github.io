The Core Manifesto

Why We Publish & Share These Write-Ups

Computers are meant to be tools of empowerment, creative expression, and personal agency. When an operating system fails, users shouldn't feel helpless, nor should they be forced to accept data loss as an inevitability. That's why we write everything down. If it helped us, it should be able to help you.

  • Demystifying SystemsBreaking down UNIX, APFS, and bootloader internals into transparent, actionable guides.
  • Promoting Data RightsYour data belongs to you. Every write-up tries non-destructive methods first and is honest about what can't be guaranteed.
  • Community Knowledge BaseDocumenting solutions creates an indexable, open roadmap for developers faced with black screens.
  • Open Engineering SpiritHonoring the classical hacker ethos of learning how software and hardware work under the hood.

Who's Behind the Write-ups
