Community Repair

Find a Repair Café.

Repair cafés are free community events where volunteers help you fix what you already own — and show you how, so next time you might not need us.

How It Works

Bring the broken thing and a bit of patience. A volunteer sits down with you, figures out what's wrong, and works through the fix together. Coffee optional; learning included.

  • Host Your OwnA room, a few tables, and a handful of volunteers is enough.
  • VolunteerFixers, greeters, and teachers all welcome.
