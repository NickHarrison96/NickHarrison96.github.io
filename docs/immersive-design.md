Main has one oversized heading, the exact approved introduction and existing short mission/contact copy. The content is not a card. - Reading: dark compositional veils protect text; the APFS article, code/log blocks and team biographies stay calm and opaque. No decorative layer intercepts input or moves text, links or controls. No boot intro, newsletter, fake telemetry or new sections.
- Motion: slow, bounded transform-only layer drift (42–54 seconds) and at most a small spring-following pointer-depth offset. No animated SVG filters, turbulence, blur, gradient stops, path geometry, full-screen shader or canvas repaint loop. Original SVGs are static images; animation moves their composited layers. Spatial movement is medium intensity but confined to decoration.
- Accessibility/lifecycle: retain native Motion On/Off, persisted choice and keyboard Space/Enter. System reduced motion wins at runtime. No-JS, coarse pointers and forced colors have deliberate static compositions. Hidden/offscreen/page-lifecycle states pause CSS animation and cancel pointer rAF. No flashes, shaking, cursor replacement or scroll interception.
- Performance: three bounded image/data layers maximum, no framework or runtime dependency. Measure instrumented pointer callback and active renderer task/style/layout cost; do not infer FPS or real-device performance from headless tests.
